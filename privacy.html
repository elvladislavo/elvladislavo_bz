Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026 · elvladislavo.com

This policy explains what happens to personal data when you contact elvladislavo through this website. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data is elvladislavo, [LEGAL NAME], [ADDRESS], Poland. For anything relating to your data, write to hello@elvladislavo.com.

What is collected

Only what you send. The contact form asks for your name, email address, the type of project you're interested in and your message. The form does not transmit anything on its own — it composes the message and hands it to your own email application, so nothing reaches me until you press send there.

If you email directly, the same applies to whatever your message contains.

Why, and on what basis

  • To answer your enquiry and, if it goes further, to prepare and carry out a project — Article 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps prior to a contract, and performance of it).
  • To keep a record of business correspondence — Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest).
  • Where accounting or tax law requires it — Article 6(1)(c) GDPR.

How long it is kept

Enquiries that don't lead anywhere are deleted within 12 months. Project correspondence is kept for the length of the engagement and for as long as accounting and limitation periods require.

Cookies and local storage

This site sets no cookies and runs no advertising or analytics trackers. It does use your browser's local storage for two small things: remembering whether you chose PL or EN, and remembering that you have already seen the intro animation. Both stay on your device and are never transmitted. Clearing your site data removes them.

Third parties

  • Hosting. The hosting provider processes standard server logs (IP address, time of request, browser identification) for security and diagnostics.
  • Google Fonts. Typefaces are loaded from Google's servers, so your IP address is visible to Google when the page loads. If you would rather that not happen, the fonts can be self-hosted — say the word.

No data is sold, and nothing is shared beyond what is described here.

Your rights

You may request access to your data, correction, erasure, restriction of processing, portability, or object to processing based on legitimate interest. Write to the address above and you'll get an answer. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw.

External links

The portfolio links out to websites built for clients. Once you follow one, that site's own privacy policy applies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
